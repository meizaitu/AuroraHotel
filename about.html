About Aurora

A hotel built around simple, thoughtful hospitality

Our goal is to create a calm and reliable stay where guests can focus on business, travel, family time or rest.

Our story

Designed for modern city travellers

Aurora Hotel was created as a contemporary city hotel with an emphasis on cleanliness, convenience and attentive service.

Instead of unnecessary complexity, we focus on the things guests use every day: comfortable rooms, clear communication, useful amenities and consistent service.
